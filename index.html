About Us

SMARTHEALT (SMART HEALTH d.o.o.) is a spin-off company of the Institute for Cardiovascular Diseases "Dedinje", established as a research and development center with a clear goal: to transform innovations from medicine and technology into concrete, clinically applicable solutions – products, tools, and services that improve diagnostics, treatment, education, and organization of work in healthcare institutions.

What We Do:

  • Development of medical devices, products, and auxiliary equipment
  • Personalized anatomical models from DICOM images (education and preoperative preparation)
  • Reverse engineering and production of parts for medical equipment (when spare parts are no longer available)
  • Education and training programs (including first aid programs for sudden cardiac arrest)
  • Support for clinical research and development of CRO capacity

Mission

We develop and transfer innovations in cardiovascular medicine into real clinical practice through clinically founded research, development of health technologies and digital solutions – with the aim of personalizing, more efficient and safer patient care.

Vision

To become a leading regional partner for the development and commercialization of health technologies in cardiovascular medicine, recognized for the number of validated solutions and international networking through relevant programs and initiatives.

DEBAC

DEBAC is an innovative addition to vascular clamps that enables atraumatic clamping of the aorta and other blood vessels using a balloon system. It is intended for patients with calcified aorta and preinstalled stent-grafts, significantly reducing the risk of blood vessel damage and intraoperative complications.

Cross the gap

Suction Rongeur

SuctionRongeur is an innovative surgical instrument with an integrated channel for aspiration, which enables simultaneous removal of calcified and fibrous tissue during endoscopic cardiac surgery. Continuously or automatically activated aspiration during closure of the cell reduces the need for additional instrumentation, shortens the duration of the operation, reduces the risk of embolization and improves the visibility of the surgical field.

TAVReX

TavrEx is an innovative single-use medical device intended for controlled expansion of TAVR prosthesis in cases of complications such as prosthesis dysfunction, leakage, endocarditis or cracks in dimensioning. The device enables gradual colab of the prosthesis structure through micro-balls, which facilitates safe separation from the surrounding tissue and reduces the risk during surgical explantation.

3D CARE Modeling Project

3D-CARE develops realistic, personalized 3D models of the heart and blood vessels, made using the latest generation 3D printing directly from CT and MRI images of patients. The models faithfully display different types of tissue and pathological conditions, including soft tissue and hard calcification, enabling microvascular interventions and plans for precise planning of demands, reducing risk and increasing safety during operations, and benefiting also for the education of young doctors and their training at clinical examinations.

UV CABINET

UV cabinet is a UV disinfection system based on UV radiation - UV disinfection system for hospital infection (HAI), designed to solve the problem of hospital infections that are increasingly spreading the number of newly diagnosed and lead to complications, extended hospital stays and increased health system expenditures. Our system uses UV radiation for effective destruction of bacteria, viruses and resistant microorganisms in hospital premises according to conventional methods of disinfection. This technology enables faster and cleaner disinfection consequently, significantly reducing the risk of infections and improving the safety of patients.

Smart Health Village

The Smart Health Village concept represents a modern approach to strengthening healthcare capacities, with a focus on innovative rehabilitation, post-hospital care, and preventive health—primarily for cardiovascular patients. Building on European Union principles for developing smart and sustainable areas, this model integrates improved healthcare services, digital technologies, environmental sustainability, and social inclusion into a coherent framework aimed at enhancing the well-being of our population.

Rather than relying solely on traditional rehabilitation services, the Smart Health Village creates new opportunities through:

  • Innovative models of cardiovascular rehabilitation
  • Community-based post-hospital care
  • Primary and secondary prevention
  • Health monitoring through advanced digital technologies (including the use of artificial intelligence)
  • Education for patients and the wider community

This model contributes to a significant reduction of pressure on the healthcare system, decreases the need for re-hospitalisation, improves patient recovery in a setting close to home, and at the same time supports the development of Serbia's health policy.

Medical Waste Management

Development and implementation of innovative, safe, and environmentally friendly solutions for the collection, treatment, and disposal of medical waste, with a particular focus on hazardous medical waste from healthcare facilities and rehabilitation centres, in line with the highest European and national standards.
